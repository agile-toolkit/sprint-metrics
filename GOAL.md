### Sprint Metrics — Goal

#### Outcome

Become the credible free answer to "we cannot justify paying for reporting".

#### Why this app can win it

Metrics and reporting are the first things commercial tools put behind a paid
tier, which makes this the clearest value-for-money argument in the suite. It also
sits downstream of every other app, so it gets richer as the suite gets used.

#### Signals

1. Sprints tracked per team, and length of unbroken history.
2. Reports exported or shared upward to stakeholders.
3. Data arriving from other suite apps rather than manual entry.
4. GitHub stars and comparisons against paid reporting in public writing.

#### Boundaries

- Not a data warehouse or BI tool.
- Not a surveillance instrument; team-level insight, never individual tracking.
- Never presents a metric without the context needed to read it honestly.
